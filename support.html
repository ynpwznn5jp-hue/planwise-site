My board is not showing on my other device

Planwise syncs through iCloud. On each device open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, and check you are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Then open Planwise, go to Settings, About, and read the Sync line. It says plainly whether iCloud is signed in and when the board last sent and received.

I bought Planwise Pro but it is locked

Open Planwise, go to Settings, Planwise Pro, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Pro covers every device signed in to that account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Planwise Pro. You can also tap Manage subscription on the Planwise Pro screen in the app. If you cancel, everything already on your board stays, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Will I lose anything if I stop paying?

No. The whole board is free. Without Pro, the board shows in a free theme and new calendar and document imports pause. Cards you already have stay exactly where they are, and renewing brings everything back.

Is my data private?

Yes. Planwise has no account and no server of ours. See the privacy policy.

Refunds
