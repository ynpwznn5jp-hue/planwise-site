Planwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Planwise (listed on the App Store as "Planwise PLNR") is a day planner for iPhone and iPad. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: your plans are yours, and the developer cannot see them.

What we collect

Nothing. Planwise has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third party code. We do not collect, store or sell your personal information, and we do not track you.

Where your plans live

Your tasks, days and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, Planwise also keeps them in your own private iCloud storage so your iPhone and iPad show the same board. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. It is protected by Apple, and the developer has no access to it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your board stays on the device.

What the app asks to use

  • Calendars, only if you turn on calendar features. Planwise reads the calendars you choose, on your device, to show events on your board. It never edits or deletes an event it did not create. You can also let it add an event you confirm.
  • Microphone and speech recognition, only while you hold the mic button to say a task. Speech is turned into text using Apple's speech recognition, which Apple processes under its own privacy terms.
  • Notifications, to send the reminders you set on tasks. Planwise sends nothing else unless you switch it on.

Things you paste in

If you add a calendar feed address or a web page address, your device fetches it directly from that site when you ask. If you import a document or photo to find dates in it, it is read on your device. None of this is sent to us.

Purchases

Planwise Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your purchase records. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Children

Planwise does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
